Pizza

Description

This delicious thin crust pizza dough recipe is so low maintenance, you can throw it together in just 15 minutes.

Can't make pizza without pizza dough

Ingredients

  • Yeast
  • Sugar
  • Water
  • Flour
  • Salt

Steps

  1. Activate yeast with sugar and water
  2. Make dough
  3. Knead dough
  4. Roll dough into a circle
